        Context length

        How the MLX models keep what they have read. 8-bit holds about twice as much context in the same memory with almost no change to answers; 4-bit about four times, and answers can change, mostly in long thinking. Gemma 3 always keeps a full cache.

        For models that think before they answer, such as Qwen3, DeepSeek-R1 and gpt-oss. At the limit the thinking is stopped and the model answers from what it has worked out so far. Off asks for an answer straight away; gpt-oss always thinks a little.

        When a chat gets close to the context length, older messages are summarized by the same model, the way Open WebUI does it. The whole chat stays saved here.
